How Image Classification Works

PlantCare AI follows a multi-stage convolutional neural network pipeline optimized for agricultural field conditions.

1. Image Acquisition

The grower captures or uploads a leaf photo via web camera or smartphone in JPG, JPEG, or PNG format.

2. Preprocessing

Images are resized to 224×224, pixel values are normalized to the [-1, 1] range, and color channels are standardized.

3. CNN Inference

MobileNetV2 inverted residual blocks extract spatial feature maps, passing through dense layers and Softmax activation.

4. Advisory & Action

Pathogen classification is mapped to structured symptoms, chemical treatments, and organic prevention guidelines.
